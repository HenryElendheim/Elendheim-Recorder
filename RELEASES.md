# Releases

## Elendheim Recorder v2.0

Records from your phone mic, keeps a searchable library with folders, and
exports your takes to WAV or MP3. Adds a settings menu with accessibility
options: high contrast, a live pitch display (C4, F#4 and so on), and
headphone monitoring so you can hear yourself as you record. Dark mode, no
cloud, no accounts.

## Elendheim Recorder v1.0

Records audio from your phone mic, keeps a library you can play, rename and
delete, and exports your takes to WAV or M4A. Dark mode, no cloud, no accounts.
